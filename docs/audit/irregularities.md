## Score-labeling policy

A numeric score is reported only when it is either (a) a local result labeled `HOLDOUT-DTI` with evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and paired 95% CI, or (b) `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED` with a copied submission-page receipt. The current checkout contains neither. Projections, owner-site values, filename attribution, and unverified leaderboard claims are not promoted into either category.

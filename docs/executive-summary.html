3. Why 0.2778 scored well (what the evidence supports)

  • Board fact (ORGANIZER-PUBLIC): 0.2778 is rank 13 (extradr19). The top public score is 0.3774. Public scores use the public test set only. The prize is decided on a private test set (initial round) and then rescored on expanded labels (final round).
  • Verified: the owner raster dotted_b2_prune_02778 is a strict subset of dotted_d2_8_02708. It removes 2,545 dots and adds none. Every removed dot lies within 200 m of the USGS catalogue, and no kept dot does. (evidence/registry_pair_0278_mechanism.json)
  • Verified: on the catalogue proxy (not a holdout), that deletion lowers DTI from 0.0491 to 0.0067. The proxy therefore ranks the two rasters in the opposite order to a test of new faults. Our holdout cannot rank catalogue-excluded designs.
  • Verified algebra: DTI = T / (0.2N + 0.8G + 0.2(T − M)). Deleting a dot raises DTI iff t < 0.2·DTI·(1 + t − k), and adding one raises it iff t > 0.2·DTI·(1 + t − k), where t is the credit change and k the dot's kernel mass. Checked by brute force on 80 sampled dots: 80/80 agree, closed-form error 8.6e-15.
  • Hypothesis, not verified: on a test of new faults, dots next to mapped faults earn little credit but still cost false-positive mass. Under the exact rule, removing them then helps. Testing this needs the organizer's hidden labels.
  • Unverified: that extradr19's method is this one. No receipt links the owner repositories to the board entry (IR-54-063).

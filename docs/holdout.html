Controls and nulls

Shift null (C1)

Thirty random torus shifts of the C1 dot pattern, scored per fold: mean 0.0042, SD 0.00075, 95th percentile 0.0051, maximum 0.0062. C1 sits far above this chance band.

Chance control (C0)

Count-matched random admissible cells with the same visible-catalogue exclusion. It isolates the effect of the exclusion geometry. It does not match spacing, so it is a weak control for line-shaped candidates.

Leakage canary

Single-feature AUC per fold for all 19 official bands, the SGMC layer and distance to the visible catalogue. Highest values: distance to the visible catalogue 0.756 (a withholding artefact, below the 0.90 cut), SGMC 0.526, all 19 bands at most 0.594. No feature is flagged LEAKAGE.

Answer. The 0.0028 gap is inside the detection floor of every paired comparison in this receipt. At the observed correlations between candidate pairs (ρ̂ from −0.016 to +0.188), the floor is roughly 0.0045 to 0.0095. A 0.0028 difference would become detectable only for the C2 or C3 comparisons against the control, and only if the two candidates' DTI errors were correlated at about 0.9 or higher (evidence/detection_floor.json). The two board values come from different submissions on different test splits, so they are not paired, and no holdout can resolve them.

What this holdout does not establish

  • Truth set. The withheld truth is the owner-mirrored catalogue. The hidden set is by construction absent from the catalogue and is expected to be less well mapped. This is therefore a catalogue-recovery proxy, not a hidden-test score.
  • Segments are raster fragments. The 3,199 eight-connected components have a median of 12 cells, so the independent-unit count is approximate (IR-54-021).
  • SGMC is not catalogue-identical. Only 17.8 % of catalogue cells lie within 100 m and 32.9 % within 300 m of an SGMC fault cell (DATA-AUDIT). C1 therefore recovers catalogue faults that SGMC maps, which is a real test, not a tautology.
  • No organizer receipt. None of these numbers is ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. None is a leaderboard value.

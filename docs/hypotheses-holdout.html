Five hypotheses for faults missing from the catalogue

Each hypothesis names its layer, its physical signature, the non-fault process that could mimic it, and its holdout status. Validation uses the whole-segment holdout. Where a source needs new data, the free official source is named and its reachability checked.

RankHypothesis and layerPhysical signatureMimic processHoldout statusLane and data
1 Magnetic horizontal-gradient ridges (official tmi_hg, GeoDAWN magnetics) A fault that juxtaposes magnetic units leaves a gradient maximum along its trace, even where no scarp is visible. Dikes, intrusive contacts, basalt-flow margins, and sedimentary basin edges. HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0148 [0.0118, 0.0180], paired +0.0044 [+0.0012, +0.0082] over the chance control. Canary clear (AUC 0.544). Below C1. Basis of H54-B: byte-unique, lane FAIL (81.6 % overlap with GEMSDOE40, 75.7 % with GEMSDOE46). Data: official training features.
2 SGMC state-map complement (USGS SGMC, free; this repository's H54-A rule) State-map linework more than 300 m from every catalogue cell is a candidate for an unmapped fault. Pre-Quaternary faults with no scarp, dikes and veins digitised as lines, lithologic contacts, and neatlines. HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0483 [0.0424, 0.0541], paired +0.0379 [+0.0317, +0.0437] over the control. Strongest signal here. Canary clear (AUC 0.526). Not eligible to upload: dot overlap 100 % with a sibling submission (GEMSDOE3 sgmc-gap), so it fails the lane rule. Source reachable: USGS state geologic map downloads.
3 Geodetic shear-rate ridges (official geod_shearrate, GPS/InSAR-derived) Active shear concentrates on young faults, which appear as shear-rate maxima. Interpolation artefacts of sparse GPS networks, smooth regional strain, and anthropogenic deformation such as groundwater withdrawal. HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0098 [0.0072, 0.0128], paired −0.0006 [−0.0037, +0.0027] over the control. No signal over chance. Unique-lane candidate. Data: official features. Not used for any file.
4 InSAR deformation time series (Sentinel-1 via ASF or Copernicus; new processing) Creep and coseismic offsets on faults show up as line-aligned phase discontinuities. Atmospheric phase screens, groundwater-driven subsidence, and unwrapping errors. Not validated. Requires new processing; no holdout run. Free source reachable: ASF data search loads, but downloads need a free NASA Earthdata login, which this session did not use. Obtainable, not obtained.
5 Landsat thermal persistence (USGS Landsat Collection 2) Fault zones with warm springs or fracture-enhanced permeability show persistent thermal anomalies. Land cover, irrigation, and albedo changes. Not validated. No holdout run. Free USGS source; reachability not checked this session.

Which hypothesis is validated before any slot?

Rule: validate the top candidate on a spatially blocked holdout before any slot. The holdout here blocks by whole fault segment with a 300 m buffer, which is stricter than a spatial block. Two candidates beat the chance control (C1 and C3). C1 is the strongest, but it fails the lane rule. C3 is byte-unique but also fails the lane rule, and it does not beat C1. Under the protocol, neither earns a slot.

No hypothesis on this page is a verified improvement on the board. The holdout is catalogue recovery, and the hidden set is not the catalogue.

Holdout values are HOLDOUT-DTI with evaluator gemsdoe54-segment-cv v1. Hypotheses are not scores. Not legal advice.

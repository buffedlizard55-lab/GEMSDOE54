1 · H1 — Catalogue Gap Relay Completion RUN · VALIDATED · NEGATIVE VERDICT

Layers: catalogue mask only. Signature: relay/stepover gaps between facing, strike-compatible catalogue tips (≤2.5 km, empty gap) + tip tangent continuations (≤2.5 km); one dot per 300 m; 200 m catalogue exclusion.

Why missing faults: a compilation misses exactly the low-displacement strands that link mapped segments; the empty gap between two facing tips is the most-likely-missed geometry, off the catalogue by construction.

Differs from registry: the only lane predicting between tips. Result (v2): HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0915 (SGMC arm) [0.0876, 0.0949], 1.50× random (0.0610), leakage canary clean (0.523); SGMC corroboration significantly helps (A−B CI excludes 0). Key diagnostic: a 300 m exclusion collapses it (0.0753→0.0042 on the 1.5 km baseline) — the mechanism operates in the 200–300 m band, the same band the 0.2600→0.2778 prune validated. Pass-2 fixed a continuation-direction bug (IR-54-021): the pre-fix one-sided geometry scored 0.1091 (2.47×) on this proxy purely by emitting less off-catalogue mass — the proxy's bias, not a better method. Verdict: negative — below corpus holdout best 0.1793; TIF generated as a unique diversity candidate.

2 · H2 — Seismicity-prioritized CGRC TOP NEXT CANDIDATE

Layers: H1 geometry + USGS ComCat + NV relocated earthquake catalog (free, official, login-free; obtainability checked — sibling-repo mirrors fetchable now, primary pull from earthquake.usgs.gov on any unrestricted machine).

Signature: microseismicity traces active fault segments; project hypocentres onto relay/continuation lines and prioritise dot placement on lines with seismicity within ~500 m.

Why missing faults: the catalogue is static; seismicity is a dynamic independent observation — a seismically alive relay segment absent from the compilation is exactly the target class. Differs from repo: GEMSDOE50's h56/h59 used seismicity for scarp-disperse/topo-lineament surfaces, not as a prior on gap geometry. Risk: geothermal faults are often aseismic — seismicity is a prior, not a filter. Cost: medium. Expected: +0.01–0.03 holdout if the prior is real.

3 · H3 — 3DEP 1 m DEM scarp-break transform on gap zones HIGH VALUE · HIGH COST

Layers: USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM tiles (free, official, login-free; the competition's own 1m_DEM_links.csv lists the ~52 tiles; blocked only by this sandbox's host allowlist).

Signature: signed slope-discontinuity (scarp break) transform with Hawker–Snoke relief criterion, intersected with the H1 gap surface.

Why missing faults: the DEM images ground directly, independent of every human compilation; young low-displacement active faults scarprelief before entering any compilation, and scarps are the canonical geothermal expression. Differs from repo: GEMSDOE46/47 fused scarps with radiometric; h59 did topo lineament scatter; a scarp-break transform restricted to gap zones (H1 ∩ scarp) is new. Cost: high (~52 × 50 MB, 1 m resampling on 4 GB RAM).

4 · H4 — Trained detector on the official Geodawn feature stack BLOCKED: LOGIN-GATED DATA

Layers: gems-geodawn-numerical-features.tif (Geodawn airborne magnetic + radiometric derivatives) + labels.tif.

Signature: U-Net regression of fault-presence probability (organizer reference solution, TverskyLoss 0.2/0.8) followed by metric-optimal dot emission (break-even bar k > 0.2·DTI ≈ 0.052).

Why missing faults: aeromagnetic structure images basement faulting independently of compilation history — young, subtle, or obscured faults still displace magnetic units. Differs from repo: GEMSDOE49/50 used the stack as hand-crafted gates, not a trained probability surface; this is the most likely explanation of the public 0.3221–0.3774 band. Blocker (named, checked): the data page redirects to login (verified 2026-10-08); specific free official source needed = a DrivenData account to download the stack from the competition data page, plus a GPU. Viability from this sandbox: NO until an authenticated download is placed in data/.

5 · H5 — Junction-node splay detection (fluid-pathway nodes) LOW COST · LOCAL

Layers: catalogue mask only. Signature: high-angle (30–80°) catalogue intersections; short (≤500 m) splay segments from junctions at strike-bisector angles, 200 m catalogue exclusion, one dot per 300 m.

Why missing faults: compilations digitize individual mapped faults, not their interaction products; geothermal systems focus on junction damage, and splay faults at nodes are systematically missed. Differs from repo: tip_stepover_r30 targets tip-to-tip stepovers; no lane emits from intersection nodes. Cost: low; testable on the same holdout instrument. Risk: junctions are on-catalogue — splay dots must sit in the 200–300 m band to carry off-catalogue information.

Not listed, and why

SGMC-complement re-weightings (lane-occupied by nine registry rasters — a new variant trips the 3-px rule against the dotted family); pure SGMC or pure continuation surfaces (lane-occupied: undercomplement, endpoint-continuation preflight); lattice/uniform (control, r13, known low). InSAR, Landsat thermal persistence, and groundwater hydraulics remain open long-horizon ideas from the prior session (sources listed on the old ledger) but are lower priority than H2–H5 given their processing cost and unverified local scene coverage.

Selection rule (unchanged)

No weekly slot is spent on an idea that has not first passed: the lane check (surface and final dots), a whole-segment-plus-buffer hide-and-recover holdout with paired 95% CI, per-feature leakage canaries, format validation, and the “beat the current holdout best” gate. Promotion to a real slot is a separate selector step within the weekly cap.

## Confounders that must be registered before any experiment

- Potential-field co-edges can be lithologic contacts, intrusive bodies, basin margins, or survey seams rather than faults.
- Conductive corridors can be clay alteration, saline groundwater, basin fill, or temperature-dependent conductivity rather than a fault conduit.
- Raster junctions can arise from map digitization, rasterization, line snapping, or multiple mapped ages; an intersection is not itself evidence of a Quaternary fault or geothermal upflow.
- DEM curvature can trace stream banks, terraces, roads, irrigation works, and erosional scarps.
- InSAR deformation can arise from groundwater withdrawal/recharge, landslides, volcanic processes, atmospheric delay, decorrelation, or processing artifacts.

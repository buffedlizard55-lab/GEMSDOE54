Verdict for gems54-undercomplement-q200.tif

Conditionally permitted under the competition rules. Not cleared under this repository's uniqueness requirement, which both files fail (see the executive summary). Not permitted if the file is treated as someone else's work. The file was produced by this repository's own build code (scripts/build_submission.py) from the competition's training labels and a USGS state geologic map layer. Re-running that code reproduces it byte-for-byte (see the submit guide). Three things are not decided by this repository and must be confirmed by the person who registers:

  1. Your eligibility under §1.3 (we cannot verify it).
  2. The certification in §1.3 and Appendix A.1, which only you can sign.
  3. The generative-AI disclosure in the narrative required by §3.2 (draft: AI_DISCLOSURE.md).

Who may compete?
§1.3		 “An individual prize competitor (who is not competing as a member of a group) must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident.” “Private entities must be incorporated in and maintain a primary place of business in the United States.” “Academic institutions must be based in the United States and accredited by a nationally recognized accrediting agency or association.” Unknown to us. Only you can establish this. Ineligible groups include DOE/NLR/FFRDC personnel as listed in §1.3.
Must the submission be original work?
Appendix A.5(1)		 “The competitor’s entire submission is an original work by the competitor and the competitor has not included third-party content … unless (i) otherwise requested by the prize administrator or disclosed by the competitor in the submission and (ii) the competitor has acquired the necessary rights …” Yes if you run the included code yourself. No for any file copied from another person's or team's repository. This includes every GEMSDOE sibling artefact and the previously published top-scoring GEMSDOE32 file. Those may be studied for learning only.
What is the submission?
§3.2 Process overview		 “You must submit a single GeoTIFF with a single raster layer at 100-meter resolution containing your model's predictions of fault locations for the entirety of the GeoDAWN study area.” Format passes (single band, float32, EPSG:32611, 3730 × 3292, values in [0, 1], NaN outside the footprint; 9 of 9 checks in evidence/validator_output.json). The competition page's format requirements are linked from the submit guide.
Generative-AI disclosure
§3.2		 “Using generative AI technology in the development of your prize submission is allowed. However, you must indicate in the narrative (not included in the word count) the extent to which, if any, you used generative AI technology and how you used it to develop your submission …” Required, and yours to file. A draft describing how this repository was produced is in AI_DISCLOSURE.md. You are responsible for its accuracy under §3.2.
How many uploads?
§3.2 and §3.4		 “You can make multiple submissions, subject to the limits specified on the competition website (three submissions per week).” “…each participating entity … may submit more than one set of predictions for automated scoring … up to three per week … By the submission deadline, you must select only one set of predictions to use as your final submission …” Limit applies to you. This repository does not select a slot or a final submission (protocol: do not pick submissions). Confirm your upload count on the DrivenData submission page; the 63 public repositories are not themselves submissions.

Code for finalists
§3.5		 “Their solution's complete code assets and documentation … should be able to sufficiently reproduce the winning results and generate predictions on new data samples.” Supplied for this file: the builder, the receipt registry/gems54-undercomplement-q200.build.json, and the hash check in the submit guide. Finalist status is not yet reached.
Public elements
Appendix A.4		 “The elements of the submission that are designated as public will become publicly available as part of this prize.” Note. This site publishes the TIF and its receipts publicly. Confirm that this is compatible with what you designate as public in your submission package.
Training data terms
§3.3 and the competition data page		 §3.3: the training labels “were obtained from the INGENIOUS project's Great Basin Regional Dataset Compilation.” The competition page lists the labels as from the USGS Quaternary Fault and Fold Database and NLR/USGS experts (see the irregularity below). To verify before publishing. This repository mirrors the training labels (data/grid/labels.tif) and a derived USGS SGMC raster. Check the competition's data licence terms before keeping the label mirror public. This is flagged as IR-54-031.

Label-provenance inconsistency (flagged)

The official text names two different sources for the training labels. §2 says: “The labels for this prize come from the USGS Quaternary Fault and Fold Database and from a set of newly identified faults labeled by geology experts.” §3.3 says: “These labels were obtained from the INGENIOUS project's Great Basin Regional Dataset Compilation” (doi:10.15121/1881483). The DrivenData labels section cites both. This does not change the upload gate, but it matters for any claim about what the labels contain. It is recorded as IR-54-030.

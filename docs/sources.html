Source rules

What “verified” means here

  • 01Read the source itself, not just a citation or a sibling README.
  • 02Record the exact dataset/version, license, footprint coverage, retrieval date, and checksum.
  • 03Distinguish checksum integrity from origin authentication. A local hash does not establish that bytes came from the organizer.
  • 04Distinguish owner-reported scores from organizer receipts and local HOLDOUT-DTI.
